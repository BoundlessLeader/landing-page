Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Living Ashram LLC, doing business as Boundless Living Academy ("BLA," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and handles personal information when you visit the Boundless Leader marketing website at boundlessleader.com. By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

This policy applies solely to boundlessleader.com. A separate privacy policy governs the Boundless Leader web application (app.boundlessleader.com).

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Waitlist Sign-Ups

When you join our waitlist, we collect your email address.

1.2 Founding Practitioner Applications

When you submit a Founding Practitioner application, we collect:

  • Name, email address, and phone number.
  • Professional background, including credentials, certifications, and years of practice.
  • LinkedIn profile, professional website, and social media handles (where provided).
  • Information about your practice methodology, client work, and professional philosophy.
  • All responses submitted through the application form.

1.3 Technical and Usage Data

We may automatically collect certain technical information when you visit this website, including IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and referring URL. This information is used for website security, performance, and aggregate analytics only.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To send waitlist updates and platform launch communications (email subscribers).
  • To review Founding Practitioner applications and contact applicants regarding their status.
  • To respond to inquiries submitted through the website.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve this website.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

We will not use your information for purposes materially different from those described above without providing prior notice and, where required, obtaining your consent.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We use Mailchimp for waitlist and email management, and Typeform for the Founding Practitioner application form. These providers process data on our behalf and are contractually required to protect it.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of BLA, our users, or the public.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will provide notice of any such change.

We do not share personal information with practitioners, partners, or third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law.

  • Waitlist email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion.
  • Founding Practitioner application data is retained for the duration of the application process and, for accepted applicants, for the duration of their agreement with BLA. Applications that are not accepted are retained for a reasonable period and then deleted.

5. Cookies and Tracking

This website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to support core website functionality and collect aggregate analytics. You may instruct your browser to refuse cookies; however, some website features may not function properly as a result.

We do not use cookies for targeted advertising.

6. Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services that may collect or process your data:

  • Mailchimp (The Rocket Science Group LLC) — email and waitlist management. Privacy policy: mailchimp.com/legal/privacy.
  • Typeform SL — application forms. Privacy policy: typeform.com/help/a/typeform-privacy-policy.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these providers directly.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • The right to access the personal information we hold about you.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate information.
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal exceptions.
  • The right to opt out of marketing communications at any time by clicking "unsubscribe" in any email or by contacting us directly.

California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know, the right to delete, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@boundlesslivingacademy.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Visitors

This website is accessible globally. If you access it from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States, where data protection laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction. By using this website, you consent to this transfer.

10. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes take effect upon posting, as reflected in the revised Effective Date above. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
